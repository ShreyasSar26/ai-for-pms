## Observations from the market scan

- **Volume is real but titles vary widely** — "AI Product Manager," "Product Manager, AI," "Responsible AI Manager," and plain "Product Manager" postings that list AI/ML/LLM/RAG as required skills. Search broadly (see queries below), not just the exact phrase "AI Product Manager."
- **Seniority skews high** globally at large tech companies (Principal/Staff level at Amazon, LinkedIn, Salesforce, Cisco) but **India has a wide range**, including entry-level (0–1 yr) roles, reflecting a bigger junior AI-PM hiring wave locally.
- **Healthcare, fintech, and enterprise SaaS** are hiring aggressively for "Responsible AI" and "AI-enabled" product roles, not just pure AI-native startups.
- Skills most frequently listed across postings: **LLM, RAG, product roadmap/lifecycle, AI/ML fundamentals, agile, generative AI, AI governance** — directly matching the framework in [01-Skill-Framework.md](01-Skill-Framework.md).
