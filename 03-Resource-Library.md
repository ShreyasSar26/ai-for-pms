## Books

- *Building AI-Powered Products* — Marily Nika
- *Inspired* — Marty Cagan (foundational PM thinking, still the base layer under any AI specialization)
- *Prediction Machines* — Ajay Agrawal, Joshua Gans, Avi Goldfarb (economics of AI decision-making, good for strategy framing)

## Communities & Conferences

- Sequoia Capital "AI Ascent" (annual, talks published on YouTube)
- Local/virtual Product Management + AI meetups (search Meetup.com / Luma for "AI Product Management" in your city)
- Reforge and Maven cohort alumni Slack/Discord communities (join if you take a paid course — the network is often as valuable as the content)

## Hands-on Tools to Practice With

- **Prompting/chat:** ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini (compare behavior across all three)
- **RAG/no-code AI:** NotebookLM, Retool AI, Bubble + OpenAI API
- **Agents/automation:** n8n, Zapier AI, LangChain templates
- **Analytics/evals:** Any spreadsheet for a manual eval set to start; graduate to a proper eval tool once you understand the mechanics
